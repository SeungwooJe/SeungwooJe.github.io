We are the Immersive Design Group, a research group specializing in making, developing, and designing new experiences in the field of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). We are in the School of Design at SUSTech (Shenzhen, China).

Our Research

We are interested in designing at the intersection of human and computer, hardware and software, virtual and real.

Our research vision is to enrich and diversify the interaction between users and computers by creating novel experiences with emerging technology. Our research involves understanding users in relation to the new types of interfaces, designing appropriate interactions using design methods, and developing unique devices as well as systems. Current research topics include VR/AR/MR, Haptics, Multimodal Interaction, and Data Physicalization.
